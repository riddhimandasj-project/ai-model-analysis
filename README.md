# AI Model Analysis

## About This Project

This repository is a beginner-friendly exploration of artificial intelligence, model behavior, and the quality of AI-generated answers.

The goal is to learn how to evaluate AI responses systematically instead of assuming that a confident answer is always correct.

## Research Questions

- How does the wording of a prompt affect an AI response?
- How often do different responses contain factual errors?
- Which responses are clearer and more useful?
- How can people check AI-generated information critically?

## First Experiment: Comparing AI Responses

### Objective

Compare two AI responses to the same question and evaluate their accuracy, clarity, relevance, and usefulness.

### Method

1. Choose a question with an answer that can be checked against reliable sources.
2. Ask two AI systems the same question, keeping the prompts as similar as possible.
3. Record both responses.
4. Verify important factual claims using reliable references.
5. Compare the responses using the criteria below.

### Evaluation Criteria

- **Accuracy:** Are the factual claims correct?
- **Clarity:** Is the explanation easy to understand?
- **Relevance:** Does the answer address the question?
- **Evidence:** Can important claims be verified?
- **Limitations:** What is missing, uncertain, or potentially misleading?

## Experiment 01: Does Prompt Detail Affect AI Responses?

### Objective

To explore how prompt wording affects the detail, readability, and usefulness of AI-generated answers.

### Method

I asked an AI the same question, "Why do people procrastinate?", using two prompts:

- **Prompt A:** A short, direct question.
- **Prompt B:** A detailed instruction requesting psychological explanations, everyday examples, practical suggestions, and a distinction between established findings and general explanations.

I compared the responses based on clarity, usefulness, presentation, and factual reliability. I also checked factual claims against reliable sources.

### Observations

- **Prompt A:** The response was easier to scan and included useful examples, diagrams, animations, tables, and a flowchart.
- **Prompt B:** The response was more detailed and provided a more extensive explanation.
- **Readability versus detail:** The longer response offered more information, but additional detail did not necessarily make it easier to read.

### Initial Results

- **Clarity:** My initial rating favored Prompt B, although my written observation suggests Prompt A was easier to read. This inconsistency needs further review.
- **Usefulness:** Prompt A was rated more useful in this particular comparison.
- **Factual verification:** I checked factual claims against reliable sources. This does not establish that every claim was correct.

### Conclusion

In this single comparison, the detailed prompt produced a more extensive answer, while the short-prompt response was easier to scan and offered useful visual explanations. The results suggest that response length, readability, and usefulness should be evaluated separately.

This experiment is based on one question and one comparison. It cannot establish a general rule about prompt quality or AI performance.

### Limitations

- Only one question was tested.
- The evaluation was subjective.
- Visual presentation may depend on the AI system and interface.
- Further tests are needed to determine whether these observations are repeatable.
